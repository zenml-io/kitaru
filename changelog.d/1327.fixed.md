- The Logfire and Phoenix importers no longer store Pydantic AI's `model_request_parameters` attribute, which lists the tools offered to the model, as the call's model parameters.
- Model-swap shortens oversized tool-call ids consistently in rebuilt requests without changing recordings, and its report no longer assumes instructions are missing when no system or developer message was recorded.
- Model-swap replays keep each recorded call's `tool_choice`, `parallel_tool_calls`, `response_format` and strict function schemas in both the chat-completions and Responses API requests, and skip a call with a counted reason (`unsupported_request_control`, `unsupported_tool`) when a control or an offered tool cannot be sent faithfully instead of dropping it. Functions without parameters are offered with an empty schema, and recorded plain-text answers that happen to be valid JSON stay text. The Langfuse importer keeps `strict` and parameterless functions in `kitaru.tool_definitions`.
- The model-swap spend cap now holds for the request actually sent: `extra_body` can no longer override the model, messages, tools, recorded request controls, token limits, `n` or `stream`; the judge reserves its own output limit; each retry reserves room for its attempt; and replies without usage, timeouts and broken connections are charged their full reservation instead of counting as free. Unknown usage is no longer reported as a measured zero cost.
- The model-swap best mix weights each step and its costs by how much recorded traffic its sampled calls stand for, for both the mix and today's cost, so a mix that picks one candidate everywhere costs what that candidate costs. Each step reports attempted, answered, failed and not-run calls per candidate, and a candidate that fails on more than a fifth of a step's calls (on all sessions or on the held-out ones) is not picked for it. Each step also says whether today's cost comes from the reference replay or the recording, and a mix of both is labeled.
- Model-swap **Best for** ranks only candidates that answered at least 80% of their calls, judges "within 10 points of today's model" on the calls both the candidate and today's model answered, shows answered-of-attempted calls next to each pick, and lists candidates that answered too few calls under "Needs more measurement" instead of naming a budget-starved candidate closest, cheapest and fastest on one answer.
- Model-swap counts a malformed candidate reply (for example a non-list `tool_calls`, or tool-call arguments that are not a JSON object in native, Hermes, JSON-content or Responses replies) as a `tool_call_parse_error` instead of aborting the run or accepting the arguments as text. Decision plans ask about a required nullable argument recorded as `null`, with `null` as one of the options, instead of labeling the call fully decided without asking.
