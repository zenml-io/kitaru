- The `kitaru/openai-post-import-insights` analyzer keeps model-written card copy whenever a card passes validation and falls back to deterministic wording per card instead of discarding every card at once. The editor no longer writes page framing, and card copy may restate numbers taken from the card's own facts or chart, keeping their unit (a count cannot ground a percentage or a duration), plus measurable comparatives and word quantities; invented numbers, causal claims, subjective quality judgments, negated or unsupported outcome claims are still rejected. A model request that fails or times out now fails the analyzer task instead of silently returning deterministic cards.
