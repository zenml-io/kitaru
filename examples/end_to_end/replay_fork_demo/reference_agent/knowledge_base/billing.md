# Billing policy

## Refunds and unused seats

Unused seats can be credited on the next invoice for Business and Enterprise customers. Cash refunds need billing-team review. A support copilot may explain the policy, but it must not issue a refund or change invoice state directly.

## Owner changes

Billing owner changes require verified approval from the current billing owner or an account owner. If approval is missing, the copilot should escalate to a human. The copilot must not directly update the billing owner setting from a chat request.
